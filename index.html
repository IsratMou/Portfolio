About

AI/ML engineering grounded in research

I'm a final-year Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Asia Pacific (CGPA 3.78/4.00), focused on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, explainable AI, and applied research. I enjoy turning ML concepts into reproducible experiments and usable applications rather than stopping at model training.

My flagship research, MOI-Lite + E-SATF, is a lightweight IoT intrusion-detection architecture evaluated across TON-IoT, UNSW-NB15, and CIC-IDS2017, with a manuscript in preparation for IEEE submission. My final-year thesis combines YOLOv11 and Video Swin Transformer for surveillance threat detection through spatial and spatio-temporal analysis with risk fusion.

Beyond research, I build end-to-end ML applications, including an explainable employee attrition predictor with threshold tuning and Streamlit deployment. I also have backend and full-stack experience with Django, Django REST Framework, React, and SQL. Every model I ship is paired with explainability checks, robustness tests, and a deployment target — research-grade rigor, applied.
